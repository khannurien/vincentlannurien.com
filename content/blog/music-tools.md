## Discover

- [Disfrogs](https://www.disfrogs.com/) -- "vinyl web radio that [...] lets you explore vinyl releases from around the world"
- [Radio Garden](https://radio.garden) -- "Radio Garden invites you to explore live radio from around the world."
- [scroll.fm](https://www.scroll.fm/) -- "The Sound of Reddit. Pick a subreddit. Get lost in the music." ~ Turns subreddits into non-stop radios

## Edition & Production

- [AudioMass](https://audiomass.co/?multitrack=1) -- "AudioMass is a free, open source, web-based Audio and Waveform Editor." ~ Now with multitrack features
- [Nightingale](https://nightingale.cafe/) -- "Turn any song into karaoke. A self-contained party game that separates vocals, transcribes lyrics, and plays it all back with word-level sync and pitch scoring."

## Playlists
- [cosine.club](https://cosine.club/) -- "electronic music similarity search engine" ~ You can create and share playlists, and the site provides a free API
- [Tracklist Matcher](https://matcher.shovelfordiscogs.com/) (Shovel for Discogs) -- "Paste a tracklist in any format. Get the Discogs release for every track." ~ You can stream the audio tracks from the generated playlist page
